Privacy Policy — Perlune

Last updated: 25 October 2026

The short version

Perlune does not collect your data. Your photos are edited on your iPhone. Perlune has no servers, no accounts, no analytics service, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits of any kind.

One exception, and it is yours to choose. A few optional features can send a sentence you type, or a photo, to Apple's Private Cloud Compute, or what you type to Apple's Image Playground. Each asks you first, nothing is sent if you decline, and Apple states that Private Cloud Compute keeps nothing after it answers. It is described in full below, so that it can be checked rather than trusted.

What Perlune does with your photos

Everything is edited on your device. A photo leaves it only if you ask an optional feature to send it (see "Optional features that use Apple's servers" below).

  • Beautification, filters, retouching, colour grading, collage and every other edit run locally using Apple's own on-device frameworks (Vision for face detection, Core Image and Metal for image processing). No video frame, no face landmark and no derived face measurement is ever transmitted off your iPhone, and a photograph only in the one case described below.
  • Perlune has no server. The developer operates no backend, no API and no storage service for this app, and receives nothing you send to Apple.
  • Nothing is uploaded in the background. There is no sync, no cloud backup performed by Perlune, and no "improve the product by sharing images" option — because there is nothing behind such an option to share with.
  • Fun Masks finds where a face is — the eyes, the nose, the mouth — entirely on your device, so it can place a pair of ears or a pair of glasses in the right spot. Those positions exist only in memory while a frame is being drawn: they are never written to a file, never attached to a photograph you save, and never sent anywhere. Perlune does not recognise who anybody is. It cannot tell one face from another, it does not try to, and there is no face database in this app or behind it.

Optional features that use Apple's servers

On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (and, for the sentence and photo features, iOS 27), Perlune offers a few optional features built on Apple's own frameworks. On other iPhones they are not shown.

  • Apple, not Perlune. These requests go from your iPhone to Apple through Apple's frameworks. Perlune has no server in between and receives nothing. Apple states that Private Cloud Compute uses your data only to answer the request, keeps none of it after the response, and that it is not available to Apple staff. Apple's Apple Intelligence terms and privacy policy govern that processing.
  • A daily allowance applies, set by Apple and tied to your iCloud account. Perlune cannot read your account; it only knows whether the allowance is nearly or fully used.
  • Nothing is added to your photo. Text a model writes is never put into a photo or its edit record; the record notes only that a change was proposed from a sentence or a photo, and where it was answered.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time: camera screen → info button → About → Forget my consent. Each feature then asks again before sending anything.

What is stored on your device

Perlune keeps a small amount of data locally so the app is usable between launches. All of it stays on your iPhone, and all of it is removed when you delete the app:

  • Your custom presets, custom filters and custom stickers.
  • Your favourite filters, and your preference settings (for example whether the watermark is on).
  • An unfinished edit, so that reopening the app returns you to where you left off.
  • Whether you agreed to each optional feature's consent screen, and pictures you made in Image Playground and kept.
  • Nothing else. There is no profile, no history of what you photographed, and no usage log that leaves the current app session.

Accounts

There are none. Perlune does not ask you to register, does not have a sign-in screen, and cannot read the Apple Account your device is signed in to.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

  • No analytics service. Perlune integrates no analytics vendor of any kind.
  • No advertising. Perlune shows no advertisements and contains no advertising network code.
  • No tracking. Perlune does not read the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), the Vendor Identifier (IDFV), or any other device identifier, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. Perlune does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has no tracking to request permission for.
  • No crash-reporting vendor. If you have opted in to sharing diagnostics with Apple in iOS Settings, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports through Apple's own systems, under Apple's privacy policy. Perlune itself sends nothing.

Purchases

Perlune offers an optional premium upgrade: a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock.

  • Payment is handled entirely by Apple. The transaction is between you and Apple. Perlune never sees your payment details, never transmits a receipt anywhere, and creates no account for you.
  • Your purchase is verified on your device, using the signed transaction iOS provides. There is no validation server, because there is no server.
  • The developer receives aggregate sales reports from Apple, through App Store Connect, under Apple's own privacy policy — the same arrangement as any paid app on the App Store. These reports do not identify you to the developer.
  • Subscriptions renew through your Apple Account and can be managed or cancelled at any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Sharing a photograph to another app

When you tap Share, iOS presents its own system share sheet and you choose the destination. The photograph is handed by the operating system directly to the app you picked. It does not pass through Perlune's servers, because Perlune has none. Once a photograph reaches another app or service, that company's privacy policy governs what happens to it — not this one.

Children

Perlune is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children — because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data a company holds about you. The developer of Perlune holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, export or delete on request. The data described under "What is stored on your device" is yours, on your own hardware, and you can remove all of it by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Perlune ever transmits anything off your device beyond what is described here, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here rather than folded in silently. The "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
