Frequently asked questions

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. Every edit runs on your iPhone, and Perlune has no server to send anything to. See the Privacy Policy.

The app is asking for camera or photo access. Do I have to allow it?

Camera access is needed to take a photograph inside the app; photo access is needed to import one and to save the result. You can decline either and still use the rest of the app. To change your mind later: iOS Settings → Perlune.

Where is my edited photo saved?

To your Photos library, when you tap Save. You can also share it straight to another app without saving a copy first.

How do I remove the watermark?

Turn it off on the share screen. The setting is remembered for next time.

How do I compare my edit with the original?

Press and hold Compare on the edit screen. The original is shown for as long as you hold it. Every tool in the app is undoable, and undo works across tools.

The beauty sliders do not go very far. Is something wrong?

No — that is deliberate. Every beauty parameter in Perlune is capped, on purpose, so that the result still looks like the person in the photograph. Jaw slimming stops at 12%, eye enlargement at 10%, and body proportion changes at 5%. There is no hidden setting that raises these limits.

What does Auto Brighten do?

It measures how dark your photograph is and sets the Low Light Boost level it needs. It is the same develop the camera control performs — one tone curve and one noise reduction — so it can only bring out light your sensor already recorded. It cannot add light that was never there, and it does not combine several frames.

What is included in the premium upgrade?

Premium unlocks the exclusive filter and makeup content. It is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock. Everything else in the app stays available without it.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Open the premium screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Perlune. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I was charged but premium is not unlocked.

First try Restore Purchases on the premium screen while connected to the internet and signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of the purchase and we will help. Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete everything Perlune has stored?

Delete the app. Your presets, filters, stickers, preferences and any unfinished edit are stored only on your device and are removed with it. Photographs you already saved to your Photos library stay in your library — they belong to you, not to the app.

Which devices does Perlune support?

iPhone, running iOS 17 or later. Perlune is not an iPad app.

Reporting a bug

Email sang89vh@icloud.com with:

  1. What you were doing when it happened.
  2. What you expected, and what happened instead.
  3. Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  4. A screenshot or screen recording, if the problem is visible.

Please do not send a photograph of anyone else without their agreement — and note that we do not need your photographs to investigate most problems.

Feature requests

Welcome, at the same address. Requests that would mean sending your photographs off the device will be declined, however good the feature — that is a fixed commitment of this app rather than a current limitation.
